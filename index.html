About me

I am a registered psychologist with over 18 years' experience working in community health. I have worked with people experiencing a broad range of psychological and life challenges, and have developed particular interests in trauma, mental health, relationships and life transitions.

I believe that understanding a person's history, relationships and strengths is central to effective therapy. I aim to create a safe and respectful space where people can explore their experiences, develop new perspectives and find ways forward.

My approach is warm, collaborative and tailored to each person's individual circumstances, needs and goals.

My approach

I take a warm, collaborative and trauma-informed approach to therapy, drawing on evidence-based approaches including , and .

Rather than trying to argue difficult thoughts away or get rid of them, ACT focuses on changing your relationship to them, and on building a life around what matters most to you.

Read more about ACT

After something traumatic, a memory can stay raw and unfinished, so reminders of it still feel like the present rather than the past. EMDR uses a structured, gentle process to help the brain finish processing those memories so they carry less charge.

Read more about EMDR

The ways we learned to connect with people early in life tend to shape how we relate to others as adults. An attachment-informed approach pays attention to those patterns and how they show up in the relationships that matter now.

Read more about attachment-based therapy

I don't believe therapy is one-size-fits-all. I draw on different approaches according to each person's presentation, preferences and goals.

The therapeutic relationship is an important part of my work, and I aim to create a space where people feel safe, respected and able to be themselves.

Getting started

  1. Get in touch

    Send a short email or give me a call. You don't need to explain everything up front, just enough for us to start.

  2. A brief conversation

    We'll talk briefly about what you're looking for and whether I'm the right fit. If I'm not, I'll do my best to point you somewhere that is.

  3. Your first session

    The first session is about understanding your situation, your history and what you'd like to be different. There's no pressure to cover everything at once.
